# ✈️ Wander Lens – Your Smart Travel Companion


> 🌍 **Wander Lens** is a next-gen AI-powered travel assistant designed to offer safe, personalized, and immersive journeys — with special attention to **female traveler safety**, **AR navigation**, and **community-driven exploration**.

---

## 🔐 Female Safety – Built into the Core

### 👩‍🦰 Safety-Centric Features
- **Safe Neighborhood Suggestions**  
  Get travel ideas in low-risk areas using AI + crime data.
  
- **Solo Female Itineraries**  
  Smart plans for solo women including safe transit and day tours.

- **Emergency SOS with Women-Specific Resources**  
  One-tap alert + helpline links and safe space directory.

- **AI Risk Detection**  
  Real-time assessment of surrounding areas with alerts and route alternatives.

- **Women-Centric Forums & Live Location Sharing**  
  Join female travel groups and share your GPS with select trusted contacts.

- **AR Safe Routes & Cultural Tips**  
  Get visual overlays for safe walking paths, police stations, dress code suggestions & more.

- **Photo Privacy Controls**  
  Blur faces, allow tagging only after consent, and more.

---

## 🧠 Core Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Exploration
- **Gemini Integration (via GenAI.py)**: Generate smart suggestions.
- **Risk-Based Planning**: Avoid unsafe zones.
- **Solo vs Group Detection**: Classify user photos intelligently.

### 🧭 AR Navigation
- Highlights safe paths, landmarks, lighting, and foot traffic in real-time.
- Includes contextual overlays on monuments and culture.

### 📸 Smart Photo Sharing
- Face-tagging, grouping (solo/group classifier), privacy filters.
- Enhance travel memories while protecting user identity.

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Social & Community Tools
- Traveler forums
- Trip invites with approval-based GPS sharing

### 💸 Travel Expense Split (Like Splitwise)
- Smart expense tracking
- Women-friendly payment modes

### 🕹️ Gamified Adventures
- Create or join treasure hunts in safe, scenic locations.
- Rewards for exploration (partnered with women-led local businesses)
